Beneath the cinematic graphite-and-copper surface are real Video.js v10 controls, adaptive Mux streaming, caption and quality settings, keyboard and touch navigation, and reduced-motion/contrast-aware states. An interface designed to help viewers explore every frame—not simply watch it pass.
